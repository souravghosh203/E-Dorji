Frequently asked questions

Is this a live tailoring service?

E-Dorji is currently a website preview. Tailor profiles, ratings, orders, prices and progress are examples. No orders or payments are processed.

How do I find an artisan?

Use the search in the top bar to see matching tailor names, specialties and locations. The listed artisans are demo profiles.

Can I apply to become a tailor?

Yes. Open Become a tailor and complete the application form. This preview validates the form but does not submit it to a server.

Contact and order support

This preview does not have a connected support inbox. For an existing garment order, please contact your tailor directly; E-Dorji does not process orders yet.

Privacy policy

This website is a prototype. The login, registration and tailor application forms are not connected to a server and do not save or send the information you enter.

Terms of service

This preview is provided to demonstrate the E-Dorji experience. Tailor listings, reviews, prices, availability and order progress are illustrative and are not offers for a real service.
